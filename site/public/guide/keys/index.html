Keyboard shortcuts

Press [?] in an editor for its own list, which is built from the same key map. [⌘] is Command, [⌥] is Option, [⇧] is Shift. On Windows, [⌘] is Ctrl and [⌥] is Alt. A plain letter acts on the selected track. [⌥] means all tracks. MD means the Machinedrum Editor only, MM the Monomachine Editor only.

Selecting steps (MD)

Select one step or a block of steps across tracks, then copy, cut, paste or duplicate it. The selection is outlined and stays when you change pattern, so it is also where a paste goes. Each operation is one undo step. To copy one step to another: [⌘] click it, [⌘] [C], [⌘] click the step it goes to, [⌘] [V] (or Copy and Paste on the LCD, which act on the selection while there is one). Right-click a step for its menu: trig, accent, slide, copy, cut, paste, duplicate, clear and the every 2nd / 4th fill. [Delete] clears only selected steps; Clr on the LCD clears the page shown. The tables below are made from the editors' own key maps, as the [?] list is.

Playing

A S D F G H J K LMD: Play the selected track: white keys C D E F G A B C D, from any workspace. ROM and RAM-P machines are pitched (PTCH, 3 steps a semitone; given back when the key is let go), others play at their own pitch. While recording: records the trig
MM: Play the selected synth track: white keys C D E F G A B C D, real notes on its MIDI channel, from any workspace. While live recording the machine records them
Z / XMD: Octave down / up, −2 to +2
MM: Octave down / up, −4 to +6: every note 0-127 (now 0: A is C-3, MIDI 48). Perform: the Octave stepper moves the on-screen keyboard over the same notes
C / VVelocity down / up: 20 40 60 80 100 127
W E T Y U O PMM: The black keys above them: C♯ D♯ F♯ G♯ A♯ C♯ D♯, so the two rows play every semitone

Selected track

RMD: Randomise the selected track: on Kit a fresh random sound (MUTATE, from the sound before the trial); everywhere else a new GEN variation, a new seed or random hits and rotation
MM: Randomise the selected track: on Kit a fresh random sound (MUTATE, from the sound before the trial); everywhere else a new GEN variation: a new seed, or random hits and rotation
⌥ ← / →MD: Pattern: rotate the selected track one step earlier / later: trigs, accents and locks, wrapping at the length. Presses while ⌥ is down are one undo step. The one Alt that is not "all": FUNCTION + arrows on the machine
MM: Pattern: rotate the selected track one step earlier / later: notes, slides and locks, wrapping at the length. Presses while ⌥ is down are one undo step. The one Alt that is not "all": FUNCTION + arrows on the machine
MMute or unmute the selected track
↑ / ↓MD: Select the previous / next track (a focused value keeps ↑ / ↓ for itself). Pattern with selected steps: move the selection a track
MM: Select the previous / next track of the side shown (a focused value keeps ↑ / ↓ for itself). Pattern with selected steps: move the selection a track

All

⌥ RMD: Randomise every track: on Kit the whole kit, everywhere else every track's GEN spec over the whole pattern
MM: Randomise every track: on Kit every synth track's sound, everywhere else every GEN spec of the side shown over the whole pattern
0Unmute and unsolo every track
⌥ Delete / BackspaceMD: Pattern: clear the whole pattern: every track's trigs and locks
MM: Pattern: clear the whole pattern: every track's notes, slides and locks (one undo step)
⌥ MMute every track; when none is audible, unmute every track

Transport

⇧ SpacePause: the machine holds its step; again (or ▶) goes on from it, STOP ends it (also ⇧-click ▶)
⌘ .MD: Panic: every voice off (the machine's STOP twice)
MM: Panic: every note off (All Notes Off on each track's channel) and STOP
- / =Hold: the tempo 10 % slower (-) or faster (=) while held, as the TEMPO menu's nudge
Delete / BackspaceMD: While live recording, hold: erase the selected track's trigs as they play (EXIT/NO + its TRIG key on the machine). With a track value pressed: that parameter's locks instead (EXIT/NO + its DATA ENTRY knob)
MM: While live recording, hold: erase the selected track's notes as they play (EXIT held on the machine)
SpacePlay / stop (paused: go on from the step)
⌥ SpaceMD: Live recording (RECORD + PLAY): Alt + play, the other Alt that is not "all"
MM: Live recording (RECORD + PLAY): Alt + play, the other Alt that is not "all". Again: recording off
T / B (MM: B)MD: Tap tempo (the average of the last taps; B as in the Monomachine Editor)
MM: Tap tempo (the average of the last taps; T plays F♯ here).
⇧ BTap tempo: ⇧B taps on every workspace of both editors
← / →MM: Step recording: ← a step back, → on (a rest); Delete clears the step, Esc: done

Machine picker

SpaceMD: In the machine picker: hear the focused machine on the track; Enter keeps it, Esc puts the old one back

Mouse: Steps

stepMD: Click an empty step: a trig there. Drag: every step crossed becomes what the first became (one undo step)
trigMD: Click a trig: select it (it stays selected: Delete removes it, ← → move it). Double-click: remove it (one undo step)
⇧ Page keyMD: Click: the previous page (a plain click: the next)
wheel on a stepMD: Move its lock in the lane's parameter, 4 a notch (⇧: 1)
⌘ stepMD: Click: select the step (⌘V pastes there). Drag: select steps × tracks. Drag the selection: a copy where you let go
⌘ ⇧ stepMD: Click: extend the selection to the step
step rulerMD: Click or drag: select steps of the selected track (down over the grid: more tracks); ⇧-click extends
right-click a stepMD: The step menu: trig, accent, slide, copy, cut, paste here, duplicate, clear, fill every 2nd / 4th from it; on a selected step for the whole selection (the ⋯ on the selection; Ctrl-click on a Mac; the Menu key on a focused step)
⇧ stepMD: Click: accent
⌥ ⇧ stepMD: Click: slide

Mouse: Roll

drag a note's endMM: The note's length (a NOTE OFF where it ends; a MIDI note's LEN, 6 a step); new notes get it too. Up to the track's next trig, past the pattern's end onto its start
⌘ stepMM: Click (roll or trig rows): select the step (⌘V pastes there). Drag: select steps. Drag the selection: a copy where you let go
⌘ ⇧ stepMM: Click: extend the selection to the step (on another track's roll: the tracks between too)
step rulerMM: Click or drag: select steps of the selected track; ⇧-click extends
right-click a stepMM: The step menu: note on / off, NOTE OFF, a trigless trig, the envelopes a trig fires (Amp, Filter, LFO), slide, swing, a chord note (roll), copy, cut, paste here, duplicate, clear, fill every 2nd / 4th from it; on a selected step for the whole selection (Ctrl-click on a Mac)
⇧ rollMM: Click: a chord note on the step
rollMM: Click an empty step: a note there, as long as the Len key says; drag it up or down for its pitch, sideways to paint that note on every empty step crossed (the last one as long as the Len key says; one undo step).
⌥ rollMM: Click: delete a note, the earlier note runs on to the next trig or NOTE OFF, as on the machine; no NOTE OFF is written or taken away (drag on: every step crossed loses its notes, one undo step), or a NOTE OFF on an empty step
noteMM: Click a note: select it (it stays selected: ↑ / ↓ its pitch, ⇧ an octave, Delete removes it). Double-click: delete it (one undo step)
