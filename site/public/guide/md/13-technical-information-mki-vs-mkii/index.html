13Technical information, MKI vs MKII

MD manual pp.82-83

SPECIFICATIONS #

MD manual p.82

The editor keeps to the limits of the machine. It refuses what the machine cannot hold, and says why.

ItemLimit
Tracks16
Patterns128, in 8 banks of 16
Pattern length16, 32, 48 or 64 steps
Kits64
Songs32, of up to 256 rows each
Globals8
Parameter locks64 locked parameters in a pattern
Sample slots48 ROM slots and 4 RAM slots

DIFFERENCES BETWEEN THE MKI AND MKII MODELS #

MD manual p.83

Pattern length

The MKII model holds patterns of up to 64 steps. The editor offers these lengths.

Not in the editor yet: The editor does not offer the MKI model, whose patterns are 32 steps long at most.

Sampler specifications

The editor's Sampler has 48 ROM slots and 4 RAM slots.

Not in the editor yet: The MKI model has 32 sample slots. The editor offers the MKII model only.

Design

The MKI machine is silver, the MKII black. The editor gives its panel either colour. Open the engine menu (the EMU OS entry in the LCD) and choose PLATE: MKI or PLATE: MKII to switch. The choice is kept for next time.

The choice is kept for the next time. It changes the colour only.
