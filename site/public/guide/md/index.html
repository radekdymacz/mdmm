MDMachinedrum Editor guide

Version 0.5.1

The chapters follow the Machinedrum manual, in its order. Each one says what that part of the machine does and how you do it in the editor. To install the editor and add your ROM, see the user guide.

  1. 1Introduction, front panel and connectorsThe editor's page in place of the front panel: workspaces, the LCD, the transport, the track rail and the connections to your audio and MIDI.
  2. 2LCD user interface and quick startWhat each field of the LCD shows, how windows open and close, and a first session: load a kit, place trigs, play and tweak.
  3. 3Sound synthesis: kitsThe kit and its sixteen machines: load, save, name, copy and clear kits, choose machines, relate tracks and edit machine parameters.
  4. 4Track effectsThe amplitude modulator, EQ, filter and sample rate reducer that every track has, and how to set and lock them.
  5. 5Routing pageDistortion, volume, pan, the delay and reverb sends and the LFO controls of each track, on the Sound and Mix workspaces.
  6. 6Stereo master effectsThe Rhythm Echo delay, Gate Box reverb, master EQ and Dynamix compressor on the Mix workspace, and how to lock them with control machines.
  7. 7LFOsThe sixteen LFOs of a kit: choose a target, a shape, a speed and a depth, and layer them to move a sound.
  8. 8Pattern sequencerChoose, record, edit, copy and clear patterns of 16 tracks, with mutes, accents, swing, parameter locks and slides.
  9. 9Song modeArrange patterns into songs of up to 256 rows, with repeats, parts, mutes, tempo changes, loops, jumps and halts.
  10. 10Global settingsThe machine's own settings: MIDI channels, the note map, program change, outputs, sync, and the SysEx send and receive.
  11. 11Sample manager, UW onlyLoad, name, hear and place samples in the ROM slots, and record into the RAM slots, on the UW machine.
  12. 12Early start-up menuWhat the machine's start-up menu does, and what to do in the editor instead.
  13. 13Technical information, MKI vs MKIIThe limits of the machine as the editor keeps them, and where the editor differs for the MKI and MKII models.

  15. 15Appendix B: MIDI control referenceTo come
  16. 16Appendix C: sysex referenceTo come
  17. 17Beyond the hardwareWhat the editor does that the Machinedrum itself does not have, or does in its own way.
